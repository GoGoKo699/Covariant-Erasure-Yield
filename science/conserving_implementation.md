# Number-conserving encoding and recovery

The information-theoretic construction allows global operations, but it need not borrow a phase reference. This page checks the conservation law for the actual accepted operator and its failure completion.

## Encoder

Initialize the logical input and $n-1$ ancilla qubits in a computational basis state of charge $w$. The two input basis states then have charges $w$ and $w+1$. A unitary that commutes with total number can map these two vectors to the chosen codewords: extend each prescribed one-vector map to a unitary within its own charge eigenspace. This requires no initially coherent reference. It is an existence construction, not an efficient circuit synthesis.

## Charges of the synthesis indices

For the odd-length central construction, let $N=n-k$, $w=(n-1)/2$, and $k\le w$. A column of $T_E$ indexed by logical $i$ and an erased computational string $e$ of charge $a$ lies in survivor charge

```math
b=w+i-a.
```

Give the auxiliary output index space $F$ the charge $Q_F|e\rangle=(w-a)|e\rangle$. All these charges are nonnegative. On $L\otimes F$, the total charge is $Q_L+Q_F$, and

```math
Q_B T_E=T_E(Q_L+Q_F).
```

The feasible matrix $X_E$ in the construction is diagonal by erased charge. Hence $H=I_2\otimes\sqrt{X_E}$ commutes with $Q_L+Q_F$, and $G_E=T_E^\dagger T_E$ does too. Its pseudoinverse has the same block structure. For the accepted contraction $D_E=H G_E^+T_E^\dagger$,

```math
(Q_L+Q_F)D_E=D_E Q_B.
```

The auxiliary labels are generated by the decoder; they do not represent access to the actually erased carriers.

## Embedding the garbage in ordinary survivor qubits

The erased-charge-$a$ label multiplicity is $\binom ka$. It fits into charge $w-a$ of $N-1$ ordinary qubits because

```math
\binom{N-1}{w-a}\ge\binom ka.
```

Indeed, $N-1=k+2(w-k)$. In the Vandermonde sum choose $w-k$ of the last $2(w-k)$ sites and $k-a$ of the first $k$ sites. The corresponding term is $\binom{2(w-k)}{w-k}\binom ka\ge\binom ka$.

Choose an isometry $J:F\to(\mathbb C^2)^{\otimes(N-1)}$ respecting these charges. Then

```math
\widehat D_E=(I_L\otimes J)D_E
```

is a contraction from the $N$ survivors to one output qubit and $N-1$ garbage qubits, all with the original number spectrum. It commutes with total number after this identification.

## Success flag and completion

Use a two-state neutral pointer, with zero charge on both flag states. The map

```math
|\phi\rangle\longmapsto
\widehat D_E|\phi\rangle\otimes|\mathrm{s}\rangle
+\sqrt{I-\widehat D_E^\dagger\widehat D_E}|\phi\rangle
\otimes|\mathrm{f}\rangle
```

is an isometry because its two flag sectors are orthogonal and their effects sum to the identity. Both terms preserve number. Extend this isometry to a unitary separately in each total-number eigenspace of survivors and pointer. Preparing and reading the neutral pointer requires no asymmetric reference. On success, trace out the garbage and retain the output logical qubit; the exact channel is the one proved in [Proposition 1](exact_recovery.md).

The even-length spectator construction and the deliberate discards used for smaller erasures are covariant as well. The surviving-qubit process may still require highly nonlocal, exponentially described control. Number conservation and efficient implementability are separate properties.
